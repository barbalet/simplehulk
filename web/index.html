How to play this console

Two players share this device. Select a unit in the roster to activate it. Select a destination or enemy on the map, then choose an action. Selecting a unit on the map does not activate it; it sets the target. Turning costs AP. End an activation before choosing another model. End the Marines phase, deploy contacts, then play alien activations. Resolve every overwatch window before continuing.

The 31×25 book map sits inside a 32×32 display grid. Outer vacuum cells are scenery. Movement animates between adjacent cells; rotations and effects are visual only. Rules, random dice, hidden contacts, items, victory and AP are handled by the compiled C engine. No automatic player or network play is provided. Alien view reveals private information: pass the device before switching views.

Relays, valves and mission gear retain their letter or number from the books. Mission collection uses Interact; Pick up is for dropped equipment. Use the item list and target ID for transfers. Overwatch is explicit: each eligible Marine may fire or decline once, in your chosen order; finish reactions to resume the alien action.
