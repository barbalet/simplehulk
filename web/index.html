Facing and equipment

Tap a Marine to activate it. Use the large action buttons below the board. Tap a floor destination, drag the active piece, or use the arrow buttons to move. Drag empty space to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.

Play guide

Choose any mission and rules version from Play. The board is the control: select a friendly piece, choose an action button, then tap the target square or piece when prompted. Turning, overwatch, weapons, grenades and equipment are in that menu. End activation there before selecting another piece. The action panel stays below the board, leaving the map clear. Tap a distant floor square to walk a route one legal step at a time; movement stops at exhausted AP, blocked routes, or a reaction.

Click an entry letter to deploy contacts. End phase becomes Finish deployment or Resume alien attack when needed. During overwatch, click a highlighted Marine and choose Fire reaction or Decline reaction. The C engine checks legality and spends AP; dragging never skips cells or bypasses reactions.

Arrow keys move the square cursor; Enter selects a unit or moves to a square, M opens its menu, Escape cancels targeting. Overview shows the whole board; + enlarges pieces again. Mission items remain on their squares or carrier icons.
