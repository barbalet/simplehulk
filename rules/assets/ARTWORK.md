# Cover artwork

Both covers were created with the built-in image-generation tool for Simple Hulk.
The FLUFF illustration uses the RULES illustration as a style reference.
Titles are typeset separately in the PDFs.

- [RULES illustration](rules-cover.png)
- [FLUFF illustration](fluff-cover.png)

## RULES cover prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: cover illustration for the original tabletop game Simple Hulk. Primary request: generic black and white ink line art of a small team of practical armored human boarding crew in an abandoned industrial spacecraft corridor, with a lurking insect-like alien shape beyond a distant doorway. Style: clean pen outlines, sparse cross-hatching, printable black ink on white paper, no color, no gray washes, no solid black background. Composition: portrait illustration, strong corridor perspective, readable silhouettes, ample white margins, no text anywhere. Crew armor is modest utilitarian pressure suits and helmets, ordinary compact rifles, no heraldry, no recognizable franchise designs, no logos or watermarks. Tense exploratory mood, no gore. The book title will be typeset separately.

## FLUFF cover prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: companion cover illustration for the original tabletop game Simple Hulk FLUFF book. Primary request: black and white ink line art of practical armored human boarding crew exploring a wide abandoned industrial spacecraft chamber with a broken observation window, pipes, hanging cables, and an insect-like alien lurking on an overhead gantry. Style: clean pen outlines, sparse cross-hatching, black ink on white paper, no color, no gray washes, no solid black background. Composition: portrait, strong architectural perspective, crew seen from behind in lower foreground, intriguing ship details, readable silhouettes. Match the pen illustration and utilitarian pressure suits of the supplied RULES cover reference, but create a distinct chamber scene. No text anywhere; titles will be typeset separately. No heraldry, recognizable franchise designs, logos, watermark, or gore.
